Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before your visit? Here are plain, honest answers.

Will the dental treatment hurt?

No. We use gentle, modern local anesthesia techniques and test that the area is fully numb before we begin. You are always in control: if you need a breather, simply raise your hand and we pause instantly.

How much will my treatment cost? Are there hidden fees?

There are strictly zero surprises or hidden charges. Following your initial checkup and digital scan, we give you a clear written estimate breakdown before any procedure starts. You decide what to proceed with.

Can I pay in installments for aligners, braces, or implants?

Yes, we provide phased milestone payments and flexible installment options for multi-sitting treatments such as invisible aligners, dental implants, and braces so you can comfortably manage payments.

Do I have to book in advance or can I walk in?

Booking via WhatsApp ensures zero waiting time. However, if you are suffering from acute toothache or a dental accident, walk directly into either our Appanthirupathi or Pasingapuram branch for urgent emergency care.

Which Madurai clinic should I visit?

Pick whichever branch is closer to you! Both branches (Appanthirupathi & Pasingapuram) maintain identical sterilization protocols, equipment, operating hours (10 AM – 9 PM), and clinical pricing.
